# KNAS 42 for iPhone and iPad (test)

Two apps:

- **KNAS 42**: watch the cable box full screen. Keeps the screen on, plays sound with the silent switch on, and can AirPlay to a TV.
- **KNAS Remote**: a native remote: TV picker, D-pad, channel and volume rockers, number pad, What's On list, Turn off all TVs, and everything else from the phone page under More.

Both find KNAS on your home Wi-Fi by themselves the first time (or type your NAS's address).
Away from home (or not on Wi-Fi), they say so and reconnect by themselves when you get back.
Hold **two fingers** on the screen for a second to open the menu: reload, change address, AirPlay.

## Installing with AltStore

1. Set up AltStore on your iPhone and PC (altstore.io has the steps: install AltServer on the PC, then install AltStore to the phone).
2. Download the newest **KNAS-42-iPhone-....ipa** and **KNAS-Remote-iPhone-....ipa** from **Releases** and get them onto the phone (AirDrop, iCloud Drive or the Files app).
3. In AltStore, go to **My Apps**, tap **+**, and pick each .ipa.
4. The first time each app runs, tap **Allow** when it asks to find devices on your local network.

With a free Apple ID, apps need renewing every 7 days. AltStore does it by itself when the phone and the PC running AltServer are on the same Wi-Fi.
A free Apple ID allows 3 sideloaded apps at once, and AltStore counts as one, so these two fill it.
